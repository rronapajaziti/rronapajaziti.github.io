Scribe

Bachelor's thesis · 2025

A platform for testing how well machines understand speech. Audio is transcribed by Google Cloud Speech-to-Text or OpenAI Whisper, then scored against a reference with standard error metrics: WER, CER, MER and WIL.

It supports Albanian, English, German and Turkish, and its dashboard compares accuracy across language, dialect, age and gender.

Front end
Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind
Back end
Django REST, PostgreSQL, Docker

RecipeLens

Mobile application · 2025

A home for the recipes that live on paper. Photograph a cookbook page or a handwritten card and the app reads it, filling in ingredients, steps and timings for a quick review before saving.

Recipes are sorted into categories with favourites and search, in English and Albanian, with a separate admin panel.
